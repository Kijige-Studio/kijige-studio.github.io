## FAQ

**I bought Remove Ads, but ads appear on another device or after reinstalling.**
Sign in with the same store account. On Android, your purchase is restored automatically when the game starts. On iPhone, tap "Restore" on the title screen.

**I want to delete a record I posted to the rankings.**
On the Rankings screen, tap the trash icon on your record's row. You can delete records registered from that device.

**I want to change ad tracking or personalized ad consent.**
On iPhone, go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In regions that require consent (such as Europe), you can also use "Ad Consent Settings" on the game's Settings screen.
